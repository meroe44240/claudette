# Humanup Chat in one page

Humanup Chat replaces Slack. It is where the team and our AI agents work together: https://chat.propium.co

## Get started (5 minutes)

1. Open the email **"Reset your password"** from Humanup Chat and choose your password.
2. Install the app: desktop (mattermost.com/download) and phone (App Store / Google Play: **Mattermost**). Server address: `https://chat.propium.co`.
3. Log in with your humanup.io email.

## Your sidebar

- **Team**: `#general` (the team), `#news` (automatic, see below), `#performance` (Maya's daily report).
- **Agents**: one private conversation with each agent. Open one: what the agent does is written at the top.

## Talking to an agent

- Write in its conversation, like to a colleague. It answers in your language.
- Each agent has its own field (Linh: pipeline stages and coding in Propium; Khoa: records and data; Théo: commissions…). Off its field, it tells you who to ask, with a **Forward** button: one click and the right agent has your question.
- `/agents` lists who does what.

## When an agent asks you something

- You get a card: the subject, the Propium record it is about (with a link), the question and a deadline.
- Answer in one click with the buttons, or reply in the card's thread in your own words.
- The agent updates Propium itself and confirms in one line. The card turns to **Resolved**.
- No answer: a reminder halfway to the deadline, then the question goes to Méroë.
- `/inbox` lists every question waiting for you, all agents, by deadline.

## Channels

- `#news`: posted by Propium, nobody writes there. Three things only: a new client or prospect meeting, a candidate presented to a client, a closed won. Enter them in Propium and they show up here.
- `#performance`: Maya's report every day at 11:00.
- `#general`: everything else for the team. Agents never post there.

## Notifications

You are notified in real time, on desktop and phone. To be left alone: click your picture › **Do not disturb** and choose for how long. Settings › Notifications lets you choose what notifies you.

## Who sees what

Your conversation with an agent is between you and the agent. Méroë sees the list of questions agents ask and the answers (to follow up), not your conversations.

## Slack

Slack stays as a read-only archive: search it if you need an old message. Everything new happens here.

Questions: ask Méroë, or write in `#general`.
